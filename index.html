About Us

United Against Drugs NYC was born as an urgent response to the addiction crisis affecting the Bronx. Our founder, David Daza, witnessed this reality both in Colombia and in New York and understood that addiction is not fought from a distance, but by being present where it is most needed.

That is why our organization combines community prevention, family support, recreational activities, and direct outreach on the streets to offer real help to those facing addiction.

We believe in dignity, empathy, and action: we don't wait for people to come to us — we go to them, because no one should face this problem alone.

Our Commitment

Mission Statement

Our mission is to confront the addiction crisis in the Bronx with direct action, empathy, and a constant presence.

At United Against Drugs NYC, we work to prevent substance use among youth, support affected families, and offer immediate help to those struggling with addiction on the streets.

We believe in human dignity, the strength of community, and that every person deserves a real chance at recovery. That's why we don't wait for people to come to us: we go to them, bringing food, guidance, recreational activities, and emotional support where it's needed most.

Our Services

Solutions we offer directly to the community. Each service is designed to support, guide, and respond to the real needs of people living on the streets of the Bronx.

  • Our organization will dispatch a team to canvass numerous streets in high-risk areas of the city, distributing flyers and alerting the community to upcoming park events in the vicinity. These events will feature food and activities for children — incentives designed to encourage greater attendance among low-income residents. We begin with young children to raise awareness regarding the harmful and dangerous nature of drugs; we provide educational guides and display impactful imagery to ensure a lasting impression, thereby making the children fully aware of the terrible damage caused by substances such as drugs.
  • Instead of waiting for people to come to us, we rely on a dedicated team to hit the streets in search of those who are truly in need of help. We know from experience that many individuals battling addiction also suffer from hunger; therefore, as an incentive, we provide them with nutritious food and basic necessities — such as a haircut, a change of clothes, or a hearty meal shared in close fellowship — as this creates a greater opportunity for them to place their trust in us. Once we have established a solid bond, our mental health support team and social workers step in to listen to their stories right on the streets, recognizing that a formal setting might make them feel uncomfortable. Many of them simply need someone to listen and to feel that they matter to others.
  • We offer recreational programs — such as sports — to provide our youth with a positive outlet, helping them take their minds off the difficulties they face. Many young people suffer from family issues, which is why they develop drug addictions; in such cases, we begin by working with the families, meeting with them prior to the individual's initial appointment. Our goal is to help families understand that recovery is a process. Once the family is ready, we initiate the detoxification process including family therapy sessions. Subsequently, we assist the individual in finding employment — as well as academic support for those who need it — while also providing daily nutritional assistance.
